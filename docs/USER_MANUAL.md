## 3. How to Choose the Right Eyewear for You

**Route:** `/how-to-choose-eyewear`

Finding the perfect frame balance comes down to four key considerations:

### A. Matching Your Face Shape
| Face Shape | Characteristics | Recommended Frame Shapes | Frames to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oval** | Balanced proportions with slightly narrower chin | Wayfarer, Round, Square, Rectangle, Aviator, Cat-Eye (Versatile) | Oversized frames that disrupt balance |
| **Round** | Equal width and length with soft curves | Rectangle, Square, Wayfarer, Geometric | Circular or small round frames |
| **Square** | Broad forehead and strong, angular jawline | Round, Oval, Aviator, Cat-Eye | Sharp geometric or rectangular frames |
| **Heart** | Broad forehead tapering down to a narrow chin | Bottom-heavy frames, Round, Oval, Light Metal | Top-heavy or thick browline frames |
| **Diamond** | High, defined cheekbones with narrow forehead and jaw | Cat-Eye, Oval, Browline, Rimless | Narrow or small frames |

### B. Frame Materials
- **Acetate:** Lightweight, rich vibrant colors, hypoallergenic, durable.
- **Titanium:** Ultra-lightweight, extremely durable, rust-proof, luxury feel.
- **Stainless Metal:** Sleek, minimalist, classic profile with adjustable nose pads.
- **TR-90:** Highly flexible polymer, virtually indestructible, ideal for sports and active daily wear.

### C. Matching Skin Tone
- **Warm Skin Tones (Yellow/Peach undertones):** Earthy colors, tortoise, gold, honey, olive, warm brown.
- **Cool Skin Tones (Pink/Blue undertones):** Black, silver, gunmetal, navy, deep plum, crystal clear.

## 4. Customizing Lenses & Submitting Prescriptions

When you select an eyeglasses frame or prescriptive contact lenses, customize your optical lens preferences directly on the **Product Details** page:

### Step 1: Select Your Lens Purpose
- **Zero Power / Cosmetic:** Clear lenses with protective hard coating for fashion and non-prescription daily wear.
- **Blue Cut / Digital Screen Protection:** Specialized blue-violet light filtration to reduce digital eye strain from computers, tablets, and smartphones.
- **Single Vision Prescription:** Custom optical lenses crafted for either distance correction (myopia) or reading (hyperopia).
- **Progressive / Bifocal:** Multi-focal lenses providing seamless vision across distance, intermediate, and near viewing zones.

### Step 2: Choose How to Submit Your Prescription
You can provide your optical prescription in one of two ways:

#### Option A: Manual Entry
Enter the numbers exactly as written on your doctor's prescription slip:
- **Sphere ($SPH$):** Measures optical power from `+8.00` down to `-12.00` in 0.25 steps.
- **Cylinder ($CYL$):** Measures astigmatism correction.
- **Axis:** Required if Cylinder is present; angle between `0°` and `180°`.
- **Add Power ($ADD$):** Reading addition for bifocals or progressive lenses.
- **Pupillary Distance ($PD$):** Distance in millimeters between the centers of your pupils (e.g., `62mm` or `PD Right: 31mm / PD Left: 31mm`). This ensures the optical center of the lens aligns with your eyes.

#### Option B: Photo Upload (Recommended)
Simply snap a clear, well-lit photo of your doctor's or optometrist's prescription slip with your phone and upload it (`JPG`, `PNG`, or `PDF`). Our certified optical team will read the values directly from the prescription before edging your lenses.

## 11. Returns, Exchanges & Warranty Policy

**Routes:** Request Return: `/order/:id/return` | My Returns: `/my-returns`

We want you to love your eyewear. If you experience fitting discomfort or prescription issues, we provide a hassle-free return and exchange process.

### Return Window & Eligibility:
- Returns must be requested within our eligible return window from the date of delivery.
- Frames, watches, and perfumes must be returned with their original protective case, microfiber cloth, warranty cards, and packaging intact.
- Custom prescription lenses cut specifically for unique prescriptions may be subject to evaluation depending on whether optical error occurred.

### How to Request a Return:
1. Go to **My Orders (`/orders`)** and select the order.
2. Click **Request Return / Exchange**.
3. Select the items you wish to return and choose the return reason:
   - *Wrong Item Received*
   - *Damaged Item*
   - *Not As Described*
   - *Changed Mind*
   - *Missing Parts / Accessories*
4. Upload 1–3 clear photographs showing the item and packaging.
5. **For Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders:** Enter your bank account details (Bank Name, Account Holder Name, Account Number) or eSewa/Khalti ID so we can disburse your refund.
6. Submit your request. Our support team reviews requests within 24 business hours.
7. Once your return is received and inspected at our depot, your refund will be disbursed directly to your account.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: What if I don't know my Pupillary Distance (PD)?
**A:** Most optometrists write your PD on your prescription slip. If it is not listed, you can request it during your eye exam, visit our guide at `/how-to-choose-eyewear` to learn how to measure it with a millimeter ruler, or upload your prescription and our support team will assist you.

### Q: Can I buy frames without prescription lenses?
**A:** Yes! Select **Zero Power / Cosmetic** lenses or purchase the frame as-is.

### Q: How do I earn loyalty points?
**A:** Simply log in to your account and shop! Every Rs. 100 spent earns 1 Reward Point. Points automatically credit once your order is marked Delivered.

### Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
**A:** Yes, if your order is still in `Processing` status. Contact our customer care hotline or WhatsApp immediately with your Order Number. Once an order is marked `Shipped`, route modifications may take extra time.

### Q: What should I do if my glasses arrive and my vision feels blurry?
**A:** New prescription lenses often require an adaptation period of 3 to 7 days, especially when switching to higher powers or progressive lenses. If discomfort continues after 7 days, initiate a return request under `/order/:id/return` or contact our support opticians for assistance.
